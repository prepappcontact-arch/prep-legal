PREP

Plan. Record. Eat. Progress.

PREP is a meal prep app for people who cook their food in batches. Set your goals once. From there you plan the week, split a cooked batch into portions that match your targets, and log the whole thing in one go.

A meal planner, calorie counter and macro tracker

One app for prepping meals, hitting your goals and achieving results.

Plan the week

Get your week sorted in a few taps. Pick the days you're prepping for, add the foods or recipes, done.

Portions

Cook a batch, weigh it, enter the number. Your targets are already set, so PREP works out exactly how much goes in each container. If two people are eating from the same batch on different targets, Smart Split divides it proportionally so both land close to their own numbers.

Recipes

Build your own recipes, with a built-in converter to help when you need it. Pull foods from the curated library or add your own, weigh it, portion it, then log it. Happy with how it turned out? Share it.

Tracking

Your day, grouped by meal, calories and macros logged against the targets you set. Search the library, scan a barcode, or add your own. Missed a day? Go back and fill it in.

Trends

A graph-chart of your progress: body measurements, macros over time, and how consistently you've logged.
