PREP

PREP is a meal prep app for people who cook their food in batches. Set your calories and macros once. From there PREP plans the week, splits a cooked batch into portions that match your targets, and logs the whole thing in one go.

14 days free, then €5.99 a month

A meal planner, calorie counter and macro tracker

One app for losing weight, gaining, or holding steady.

Portions

Cook a batch, weigh it, enter the weight. Your targets are already set, so PREP works out the grams that go in each container straight away.

Foods and tracking

A curated food library to search, plus your own. Your day is grouped by meal, with calories, protein, carbs and fat against the targets you set.

Progress

Weight and measurements shown as a trend line, so one heavy morning does not look like a setback.

PREP stays deliberately narrow. No step counter, water tracker or workout log, and nothing social. The aim is to do meal prep and tracking properly rather than a bit of everything.
